How it works

Everything happens on your Mac. Only the transcript text goes to Claude, for the summary.

1

Record

When Zoom, WhatsApp or your browser starts using the microphone, a small "Record this call?" pop-up appears. Or press ⌃⌥⌘R. Your mic is saved as "Me" and the Mac's audio as "Them", so the transcript knows who said what without any speaker-detection AI. The call window is captured as video too.

2

Transcribe, on the machine

whisper.cpp with ivrit.ai's Hebrew model, locally. Hebrew calls keep their English jargon in English letters: CRO, ROI, KPI, landing page and about 200 more, plus your own terms. English-only calls use the stock English model. Audio never leaves your Mac.

3

Summarize with Claude

A title, overview, who was on the call, key points, decisions, action items by name and open questions. Always in English, even for Hebrew calls. Your API key lives in the macOS Keychain.

4

Find it again

All calls in one library, grouped by day, with search across every transcript and summary. Hebrew search matches words with or without prefix letters. Click a line to hear that moment. Export any call as Markdown, or have every call land in your Google Drive.

First launch

Gatit is signed ad hoc, not with an Apple developer certificate, so macOS asks once before opening it.

Unzip and drag Gatit into Applications

Then double-click it. macOS will show a warning: that's expected.

Allow it, once

macOS 15: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the line about Gatit, click Open Anyway, then Open. macOS 14: Control-click Gatit → Open → Open.

Grant the permissions on your first recording

Microphone and System Audio Recording. With video on, also Screen & System Audio Recording. Until that one is allowed, calls are recorded as audio only.

macOS says Gatit is "damaged and can't be opened"

The download was quarantined before macOS checked its signature. Clear the flag and open it again:

xattr -cr /Applications/Gatit.app

Privacy

Built for personal use, and open source so you can check.

No bot joins the call. Nobody on the other side sees an extra participant.

Audio stays on your Mac. Transcription is local. Only the transcript text is sent to Claude.

Plain files. Each call is a folder of audio, video, transcript and summary files under Application Support. No lock-in.

Keys in the Keychain. Your Claude API key and Google sign-in are stored by macOS, not in a config file.

Some places require telling the other people on a call that you are recording.
